# Ownership and Escalation Template

Only role names and required responsibilities belong in Git. Personal names and contact details remain external.

| Required role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Business/system owner | Service priority, operational acceptance, and business escalation |
| Technical service owner | Reliability, application support, and on-call coordination |
| Access release/signing owner | Client build, managed signing, distribution, and rollback approval |
| Database backup/recovery owner | Backup, PITR, restore exercises, and recovery decisions |
| Account/roster administration | Staff identity, account lifecycle, and enrollment controls |
| Security/incident response | Security review, alert response, and incident coordination |
| Records-retention authority | Retention, export, printing, and records disposition |

Escalation records must state severity, responsible role, acknowledgement target, decision authority, and external evidence reference without copying contact or incident content into Git.

Agents record only whether ownership evidence was reviewed, never its contents.

Store completed records in the agency-approved system of record.
