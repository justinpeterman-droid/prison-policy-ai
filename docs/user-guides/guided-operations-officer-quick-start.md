# Guided Operations Officer Quick Start

## Sign in and sign out

Use your individual sign-in; do not share an employee number, PIN, browser, or
session. Finish with safe sign-out on every shared workstation.

## Daily work

- Use **Home** for your authorized work summary.
- Start **New Report** to collect an incident, then review its facts before
  generating any official paperwork.
- Use incident **Reports** for saved revisions and copy-only outputs. Never
  treat a copied output as a completed official record without the required
  review process.
- Complete only the populated required forms that the workspace identifies; keep
  unknown facts visibly incomplete and resolve them through the proper process.
- Follow the physical Chain of Custody reminder: it remains a physical form and
  the application does not replace its custody requirements.
- Use **Count Sheet**, **Forms Library**, **Policy Expert**, and **Account** only
  for their stated operational purpose.

## Save states

Wait for a saved confirmation before leaving a revisioned record. If a conflict
or error appears, keep your work open, note the request ID if shown, and contact
the designated supervisor or support path rather than overwriting another
person's revision.

Store completed records in the agency-approved system of record.
